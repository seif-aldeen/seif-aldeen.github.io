About Me

I'm a Mechatronics and Robotics Engineering student at Helwan National University and a freelance Embedded Systems & PCB Design Engineer. I work across ESP32 and Raspberry Pi platforms, mixed-signal and RF-aware PCB design, power and sensor interfaces, Linux services, manufacturing releases, and bench integration. I also founded and lead the Spark X Racing Team.

My work connects the full engineering path—from architecture and schematic decisions to layout, documentation, troubleshooting, and evidence-based handoff. I report validation honestly: designed is not fabricated, fabricated is not brought up, and a successful bench prototype is not automatically a finished product.
